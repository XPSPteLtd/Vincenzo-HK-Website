

You should not heavily chase “best pizza hong kong” as a core SEO keyword on your own site.
That query often has listicle / review / aggregator intent, not direct restaurant intent, so Google often prefers Tripadvisor, Time Out, Reddit, and “best of” pages for that kind of search.  ￼

Instead, you should build around:
	1.	high-intent local restaurant keywords
	2.	style-specific pizza keywords
	3.	area-based discovery keywords
	4.	brand authority / story keywords
	5.	reservation + menu intent keywords

And yes — because this is local restaurant SEO in Hong Kong, you should also make sure you are benchmarked against visible players like Little Napoli Pizzeria, La Camionetta, The Pizza Project, FINI’S, and Amalfitana Repulse Bay, because these are the kinds of businesses Google already understands as “pizza / Italian / local dining intent” in Hong Kong.
